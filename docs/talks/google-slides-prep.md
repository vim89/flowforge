# Google Slides Production Guide

> Practical checklist for crafting and delivering high-stakes talks (e.g., ScalaIO 2025) in Google Slides. Pair this with `docs/talks/speaker-notes-ScalaIO-2025.md`.

---

## 1. Planning & Structure
- **Outline before design:** Map the story (Hook → Stakes → Solution → Proof → Call to action) so every slide has a defined purpose before you touch the canvas. citeturn2search5
- **Lock theme early:** Customize theme fonts, colors, and backgrounds via *Slide ▸ Edit theme* to keep typography and branding consistent. citeturn1search5turn2search0
- **Keep slides lean:** Follow the 6×6 or 5×5 rule—limit bullets to short phrases so the audience listens to you instead of reading paragraphs. citeturn2search1turn2search8
- **Use master layouts & guides:** Edit master slides once, then align elements with *Arrange ▸ Align/Distribute* or guides to maintain a clean grid across the deck. citeturn2search0turn2search2

---

## 2. Visuals, Media & Data
- **High-res visuals > paragraphs:** Swap dense blocks of text for diagrams, charts, or icons that support the narrative; keep imagery clean and relevant. citeturn2search2turn2search7
- **Video embedding:** Insert clips with *Insert ▸ Video* and set *Format options ▸ Video playback ▸ Play (automatically)* so they run without manual clicks. citeturn0search7turn0search9
- **Speaker notes voice typing:** Use *Tools ▸ Voice type speaker notes* to capture natural phrasing quickly while rehearsing. citeturn0search1

---

## 3. Speaker Notes & Presenter View
- **Notes as script:** Toggle notes with *View ▸ Show speaker notes* and outline each slide’s beats (hook, proof, CTA) so delivery stays tight. citeturn0search1
- **Presenter view workflow:** From the ▼ next to *Slideshow*, choose *Presenter view* to access the timer, next-slide preview, and notes; resize panes for comfort. citeturn0search1
- **Dual-display prep:** Mirror the slideshow to the projector and keep Presenter view on your laptop; export notes via *File ▸ Print settings ▸ 1 slide with notes* as a backup script. citeturn0search1

---

## 4. Audience Engagement Tools
- **Live Q&A:** In Presenter view, open **Audience tools ▸ Start new** to share a question link attendees can use to submit and upvote topics. citeturn0search0turn0search2
- **Laser pointer:** Toggle the built-in laser (Presenter view toolbar or `Ctrl+L`) to highlight details without extra hardware. citeturn0search3
- **Tablet annotations:** Present via Google Meet on a tablet and tap the pen icon to sketch temporary highlights on slides. citeturn1search1

---

## 5. Collaboration & Version Control
- **Commenting & mentions:** Gather feedback asynchronously with comments and @mentions on specific elements. citeturn1search5
- **Version history:** Capture milestones through *File ▸ Version history ▸ Name current version* (e.g., “Dress rehearsal”). citeturn1search5
- **Checklist slide:** Track to-dos (assets, alt text, rehearsal notes) on a hidden slide, then omit it during the live deck. citeturn1search5

---

## 6. Accessibility & Polish
- **Contrast & readability:** Use high-contrast color pairs and uncluttered backgrounds for legibility. citeturn2search2turn2search7
- **Alt text:** Add descriptions with *Right click ▸ Alt text* so screen readers and exports remain accessible. citeturn1search5
- **Logical layout:** Maintain consistent placement and avoid stacking decorative shapes over text; rely on master layouts and guides. citeturn2search2turn2search11

---

## 7. Rehearsal Routine
1. **Silent pass:** Run through slides to verify transitions, animations, and autoplay media. citeturn0search7
2. **Notes rehearsal:** Present in Presenter view, refining timing and speaker notes. citeturn0search1
3. **Tech check:** Test the actual venue setup—dual displays, autoplay permissions, audio routing—before showtime. citeturn0search7turn0search0

---

## 8. Delivery-Day Checklist
- Launch Presenter view, reset the timer, and preload Q&A so it’s ready when you open questions. citeturn0search0turn0search1
- Insert a blank spacer slide before demos to cover transitions and give yourself a beat. citeturn2search11
- When sharing URLs or access codes, duplicate the slide with oversized typography and leave it onscreen while you explain the steps. citeturn2search8
